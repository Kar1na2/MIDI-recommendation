03 / Methodology

How the scan works

  1. Transcribe by source. A piano-and-pedal model was tried on the isolated other stem; a monophonic pitch tracker on bass; and a drum-specific model on drums.
  2. Find rising runs. Collapse attacks within 55 ms, then keep runs with at least three notes, at least a five-semitone rise, and no large downward steps.
  3. Rank triples. Search for three nearby runs. Equal note counts rank first; target-derived count shapes follow. Chords, keys, and exact pitches do not contribute.
  4. Listen back. The MIDI results are candidates, not confirmation. Verify the highlighted passage in the audio.

The target transcription has pedal events, but its opening notes overlap heavily. The passage count used for this visualization is a review hypothesis, not verified ground truth.
