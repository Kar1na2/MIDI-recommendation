02 / Evidence and ranking

A shape test, not a chord match.

  1. Three uninterrupted rises. Each contains at least three attacks and rises at least five semitones. A downward or repeated attack starts a new figure; no intervening fragment is skipped.
  2. A specific peak contour. The first figure’s peak is lower than the third’s; the third’s is lower than the second’s. This interprets your ending pattern across the three arpeggios.
  3. Most similar first. All selected passages have broken-chord-like rises. Equal counts rank first, followed by closeness to the target’s approximate 0.15 s attack pulse, evenness, and shorter gaps. Keys and chord names are ignored.
  4. Listen and compare. The isolated line is the detector’s reduction of the score. The full score lets you hear the surrounding voices. Excerpt times refer to the linked MIDI, not an independently timed performance video.
